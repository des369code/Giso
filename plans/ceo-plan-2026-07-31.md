## Vision

### 10x Check
The 10x version is the MCP/agentic AI layer — saved posts become a personal knowledge base that LLMs reason over. Every save compounds the library's value; the more content a user saves, the more valuable the product becomes to their agents. This is the moat the validation pipeline identified. It is NOT in MVP scope (user decision — Premise 4 holds: launch product serves normal people, not agents).

### Baseline
The MVP itself: Instagram-only iOS app. Share a reel → AI distills an actionable card (caption-only extraction, Claude API) → one push per day surfaces "Today's one thing you saved" 3 days after save. SwiftUI + Supabase + Claude API. Kill threshold at Week 6: 0 of earliest cohort willing to pay → don't build the full platform.

## Accepted Scope (delta over the design doc at ~/.gstack/projects/SavedPostIdea/d.desmaanzephyll-unknown-design-20260731-172742.md — the design doc IS the base MVP plan; this document is the delta record)
- Share extension renders the AI-distilled card inline at save time (instant value). **Implementation notes:** (a) the extension renders ASYNC — "Saving…" state, card arrives when ready; iOS kills long-running extensions, so the extension never blocks on the Claude call; (b) the extension shows the bare-save fallback ("We couldn't extract takeaways") inline, not just in-app; (c) extension auth via App Group shared container.
- "What are you working on this week" context field reranks the pending-card queue. **Reverses Design Open Q3** (was "Week 3 enhancement, not MVP"); accelerated to test the relevance hypothesis with real users. Rerank semantics per design doc: rerank picks which ELIGIBLE card is "today's" — it does not change the per-card 3-day window.
- Home-screen widget showing "Today's card" (shared rendering with in-app card). **Effort corrected to M** (WidgetKit timeline + App Group provisioning + timeline invalidation when the server-assigned card changes). Widget is the push-permission-denied fallback; when the user has BOTH push and widget, both surfaces show the same card — intended, do not "fix."
- Shareable card image export (stories/X-ready). Server-generated image keyed by card ID.
- **Timeline reconciliation:** accepted scope adds ~1 week to the base MVP estimate (now ~3-5 weeks). Week 2 vertical slice unchanged. Week 4 TestFlight/pay goal unchanged. Week 6 kill threshold unchanged.

## Paid Tier (user decisions, revised by outside voice)
- Context rerank + shareable export are FREE at launch (erosion accepted consciously).
- **Pricing meter REVISED: pushes, not saves.** The scarce unit is attention. Free tier: 1 push/day within a monthly push budget. Paid tier: more pushes + priority + future hooks (history search, multiple context fields, priority extraction). The 50-save cap becomes an implementation detail, not the product boundary.
- The Week 6 WTP test compares push-metered vs save-metered cohorts behind feature flags (both meters live; A/B at Week 6).

## Outside Voice Decisions (cross-model tension resolution, 2026-07-31)
1. **CONCIERGE TEST — ADDED (P1, highest-priority pre-build gate):** 10-20 heavy savers from the survey pool; founder hand-writes actionable cards from their saved reels, delivers at +3 days (text/DM). Answers "does anyone want a delayed AI card" in 2 weeks with zero platform engineering. Also tests delay preference (2/3/5/7 days) per user — the "3 days" magic number gets real data. Native build continues in parallel. If manual cards engage nobody → kill before extraction pipeline work.
2. **INGEST GATE HARDENED:** The 20-reel spike adds a server-side-fetch gate: can captions be fetched from a Supabase datacenter IP (login walls, consent interstitials, bot-blocking)? Plus: low-information-caption pathway ("link in bio" filler — garbage cards kill trust, define what a no-info caption produces) and SSRF allowlist extended to instagr.am/ig.me variants.
3. **COLD START:** First card arrives DAY 1 after the first save ("here's the first thing you saved — what to do with it"), then the 3-day cadence. Delay preference validated in the concierge test.
4. **HARDENING KEPT (3D stands):** Health cron kept — Sentry catches crashes, not a scheduled job that never ran. Eval suite reframed as dev-loop tool (fixture transcripts for prompt iteration), not a quality gate.
5. **AUTH BOOTSTRAP (implementation note):** Share extension needs a Supabase JWT without requiring app-open first. Anonymous auth from extension + account merge on app open. (Deferred while concierge test runs — manual delivery needs no app.)
